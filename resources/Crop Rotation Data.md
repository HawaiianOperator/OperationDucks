# Crop rotation data for Duck

Use the selected field polygon and a season identifier as the join keys. A season may cross a calendar year or have more than one crop. Store the source, date, resolution, quality flags and available coverage with each retrieved value. Load publisher products on demand and keep farm training records in the authorized training environment.

| Input | Variables | Sources and coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field identity and crop history | Boundary, crop and variety, crop family, planting and harvest dates, cover crop, double crop, fallow | Farmer's field records are the reference. [USDA Crop Sequence Boundaries](https://www.nass.usda.gov/Research_and_Science/Crop-Sequence-Boundaries/) and [annual CDL](https://www.nass.usda.gov/Research_and_Science/Cropland/Release/index.php) give mapped U.S. history where covered. [ESA WorldCereal](https://esa-worldcereal.org/en/products/global-maps) provides global 2021 crop and seasonal layers. Treat them as a dated snapshot. |
| Soil chemistry, texture and constraints | pH by depth and method, organic carbon, texture, bulk density, CEC, drainage, nutrients and salinity | [ISRIC SoilGrids](https://isric.org/explore/soilgrids) gives modeled regional grids. Obtain laboratory measurements for field pH, salinity and nutrients, using a consistent depth and method. |
| Climate, terrain and water | Frost and heat days, rainfall, solar radiation, evapotranspiration, soil moisture, irrigation availability, slope and flood risk | [NASA POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/) for weather history, [GPM IMERG](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/imerg) for precipitation, [NASA SMAP](https://smap.jpl.nasa.gov/) for regional moisture, [Copernicus Global Drought Observatory](https://drought.emergency.copernicus.eu/) for drought context, and field irrigation records for actual supply. |
| Vegetation and spectral observations | Planting and harvest signals, canopy cover, vegetation indices, surface heat, reflectance and quality masks | [NASA Earthdata Search](https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/) for HLS/Landsat, MODIS, ECOSTRESS, EMIT and AVIRIS-NG where available. Spectral values can support crop classification and stress investigation when calibrated against verified samples. |
| Agronomy and local constraints | Candidate crop maturity, nutrient and water needs, planting criteria, local rotation gaps, pests, diseases, weeds, residue and herbicide carryover | [FAO Global Agro-Ecological Zones](https://www.fao.org/gaez/en) for broad suitability. Use locally calibrated extension guidance and field scouting to interpret crop-specific constraints. |
| Management and outcomes | Irrigation, amendments, tillage, labor, input use and prices, yield, crop quality, erosion or cover observations, margins | Producer records and field surveys. [FAOSTAT](https://www.fao.org/faostat/) can supply country-level context. Do not substitute regional totals for measured field outcomes. |

## Training and evaluation

### Discovery and coverage checks

`python watch_agriculture.py --minutes 720` searches Dataverse, Zenodo, Figshare, and the DataCite dataset index every minute. It groups results with the same DOI, retains repository and region matches, and writes candidates to `.duck-model/agriculture-discovery.json`. Keeping a candidate in this pool does not add a crop observation to any field.

The worker retests six map selections every ten minutes. It reports points with inventory records, points with usable named crop labels, and points supporting a mapped recurring cycle as separate measures. The coarse French selection is the benchmark location. Its individual parcel is a separate test.

Public European expansion sources include [EuroCrops v2 parcel histories and linked parcel years](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC147107) and [Copernicus high-resolution cropland crop types and cropping patterns](https://land.copernicus.eu/en/products/high-resolution-layer-croplands). These sources are candidates for coverage beyond the existing French feed.

### Additional regions for named crop labels

- **Netherlands:** The [official PDOK/RVO crop parcel API](https://www.pdok.nl/ogc-apis/-/article/basisregistratie-gewaspercelen-brp-) is connected. Duck tested a published 15.645 ha parcel near 52.50035, 5.62559 in Flevoland. The 2025 onion declaration matched all five sampled points. Earlier editions are published separately through the [BRP download feed](https://service.pdok.nl/rvo/brpgewaspercelen/atom/v1_0/basisregistratie_gewaspercelen_brp.xml), and the connected feed supplies one definitive year.
- **Flanders, Belgium:** The [agriculture agency's open data portal](https://landbouwcijfers.vlaanderen.be/open-geodata-landbouwgebruikspercelen) exposes annual parcel downloads. The public WFS timed out during the test, so the provider remains a candidate.
- **Argentina:** [INTA National Crop Maps](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/486863) provide 30 m winter and summer labels for seven consecutive campaigns. Initial range reads at 33.90000° S, 60.80000° W returned soybeans in the 2024 and 2025 summer maps, winter cereals in 2023, and a fallow class in winter 2024. Wider and repeat range requests returned errors. Duck has not connected this source to the live rotation workflow.

### Public harvest outcomes for Phase Two

- **Kerbernez, Brittany, France (1978–2005):** [Published experimental data](https://doi.org/10.57745/P8NNZK). Verified the public Dataverse file API returns [117 maize yield rows](https://entrepot.recherche.data.gouv.fr/api/access/datafile/348298) and [442 grassland yield rows](https://entrepot.recherche.data.gouv.fr/api/access/datafile/348295) as tab-separated tables. Join with the treatment descriptions to interpret the rotation codes. The tables report treatment-level means, spread, and replicate counts. Keep repeated cuts, harvest years, and replicate counts distinct.
- **North American long-term maize trials:** [Dryad publication and CSV listing](https://doi.org/10.6078/D1H409). Contains rotation treatments and maize yields from 11 rainfed U.S. and Canadian experiments. The file endpoint returned 403 in the web check, so automatic ingestion remains unverified.
- **Broadbalk, UK:** [Annual wheat grain and straw yields for 1968–2022](https://www.era.rothamsted.ac.uk/dataset/rbk1/01-YLD6822). Published as a CSV package with rotation and management metadata. Automated download has yet to be tested.

Use direct file APIs with source attribution and version tracking. Evaluate on held-out sites and years. Compare matched treatments within a site and year, and report yield differences in the source units. These datasets support outcome tests for their experimental crops and regions. The selected French rapeseed–wheat–barley parcel still lacks a public harvest outcome.

These are acquisition candidates. Duck needs verified overlap with the selected field and comparable labels before adding a source to its rotation coverage. A test of recommendation outcomes requires dated farm harvest or trial results with the crop sequence, yield units, and a comparison treatment or baseline.

### Source verification · November 15, 2026

- **Brazil backfill:** [MapBiomas Collection 7.1 ImageServer](https://ic.imagery1.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/MapBiomas/ImageServer) is connected to Duck. The Mato Grosso selection returned inventory records at 9 of 9 points. The latest year is 2021, with eight soybean pixels and one other temporary crop pixel among the sampled points. The center pixel was mapped as soybeans each year from 2010 to 2021.
- **Kenya backfill:** [JRC seasonal crop-type rasters](https://jeodpp.jrc.ec.europa.eu/ftp/jrc-opendata/Copernicus4GEOGLAM/Kenya/Crop_Maps%26Area_estimates/) are connected using the numeric legends from each season's end-of-season report. The test center returned potato-dominant crops in the 2021 long rains, other land cover in the 2022–23 short rains, and maize-dominant crops in the 2023 long rains. The latest map returned six maize-dominant pixels and three other-land-cover pixels in the sample. Dominant classes can include mixed crops. Gaps between the mapped seasons leave a recurring rotation unverified.
- **Kenya 2021 short rains backfill:** [End-of-season raster and report](https://jeodpp.jrc.ec.europa.eu/ftp/jrc-opendata/Copernicus4GEOGLAM/Kenya/Crop_Maps%26Area_estimates/Short_Rains2021/03_Task_5/) use UTM zone 36N and a season-specific class legend. The connected feed returned maize-dominant crops at the test center in this season, after the potato-dominant record for the 2021 long rains. Dominant crop labels remain seasonal observations.
- **Punjab 2023–24 map candidate:** [Public research project raster](https://github.com/namrata-613/AI-Assisted-Crop-Type-or-Cropping-Pattern-Mapping-using-Multi-season-Satellite-Data-) overlaps all nine benchmark points. The archive does not provide an explicit reuse license or a reliable code legend for its published raster. Unverified pixel values were excluded from coverage.
- **India backfill:** The [northern India 2024 and South/Southeast Asia 2015 rice rasters](https://zenodo.org/records/21349862) responded to ranged requests through the file API on retry. Duck reads positive paddy rice pixels from both 30 m reference-year composites. The Punjab test returned rice evidence at 3 of 9 points. Zero values do not identify another crop, and the composites do not establish consecutive seasons or a rotation.
- **India additional dates:** [The 10 m winter rice rasters for 2018, 2020, and 2022](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24228619) were sampled and connected. One checked point had rice evidence in all five available reference years. The union of points with rice evidence remains 3 of 9. The summer and autumn rasters from this product end south of the test field.
- **India field-survey overlap:** [CIMMYT's Punjab plot surveys](https://data.cimmyt.org/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=hdl:11529/10548905) have crop and geographic fields. All 262 valid 2021 locations and 426 valid 2022 locations fell outside the test radius. The nearest was approximately 9.3 km away, so the survey did not change the field score.
- **Kenya field-reference check:** The [Helmets Kenya CSV](https://zenodo.org/records/15133324) returned 4,925 labeled field points. None fell within the 350 m test selection, and the nearest was roughly 8 km away. The [monoculture and intercropped maize raster archive](https://zenodo.org/records/18344352) marks its files as restricted.
- **Western Australia access:** The [DPIRD multi-year field classification study](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18101653) confirms the underlying paddock records are commercial. The [public Focus Paddocks study](https://doi.org/10.1071/CP14221) summarizes 687 paddock-years, but a downloadable set of crop records that can be matched to the test polygon was not verified.
- **Western Australia backfill:** [ESA WorldCereal winter cereals](https://esa-worldcereal.org/en/products/global-maps) is connected through the public [Terrascope STAC service](https://docs.terrascope.be/Developers/WebServices/TerraCatalogue/STACAPI.html) and raw point classifications. The same 350 m test selection returned a positive 2021 winter-cereal crop-group label at all 9 points. This group includes wheat, barley and rye and only supplies one mapped year, so it is excluded from annual rotation scoring.

- **Brazil:** Tested byte-range access to the [publisher’s MapBiomas Collection 11 GeoTIFFs](https://brasil.mapbiomas.org/iniciativas-e-produtos/cobertura-e-uso-da-terra/cobertura-30m/cobertura/) for 2022–2025 at the Mato Grosso test selection. All four URLs returned HTTP 403 from the local runner. The [Earth Engine clipping and export workflow](https://brasil.mapbiomas.org/downloads/baixe-mapas-recortados-geotiff-via-google-earth-engine/) remains the publisher-documented route.
- **France:** The public RPG supplies crop identities and parcel boundaries. A [field-level French wheat yield study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/remote-sensing/articles/10.3389/frsen.2026.1798106/full) reports that the underlying parcel yield survey records are access-restricted. Open Agreste statistics are aggregated at department scale and cannot validate a harvest from the selected parcel. The exact parcel yielded 100% mapped history agreement in the shape tests. No measured harvest or comparison treatment was available for an outcome score.

Phase Two can retrieve annual crop history at sampled locations from USDA, Canada's Annual Crop Inventory, and France's Registre Parcellaire Graphique. Canada's values are raster classes. France's values are declared crops from parcels that contain the sampled point. Year-by-year observations may span changes in parcel boundaries. Farm notes can provide history for a single selected plot wherever local records are available.

Additional candidate datasets for expansion include [France Crop Successions 2015–2024](https://doi.org/10.57745/6G0JTJ), [Netherlands BRP crop parcels](https://data.europa.eu/data/datasets/82096-gewaspercelen-inspire-geharmoniseerd-?locale=en), [EuroCrops](https://www.eurocrops.tum.de/), [MapBiomas Brazil agricultural use](https://brasil.mapbiomas.org/iniciativas-e-produtos/cobertura-e-uso-da-terra/agricultura/uso-agricola/), and [USDA Long-Term Agroecosystem Research](https://ltar.ars.usda.gov/data/data-access/). Match field boundaries before interpreting sequences across years and keep measured trial outcomes tied to their location and treatment.

Train tool selection on labeled questions that specify the field, prior seasons, candidate crops and decision. Assemble crop-sequence examples with verified field history and missing-data labels. For predictive models, use past inputs only, split evaluation by held-out field and later season, and compare against simple rotation and weather baselines. Report performance by region and crop, including missing-data cases and the rate of incorrect crop-sequence claims.

The dashboard retrieves soil pH and queries a public 2017–2024 CSB feature service covering Minnesota for polygons intersecting the selected point. It reads the eight majority crop classes using the service's published code labels. The contiguous U.S. 2018–2025 release is separately available on USDA's download page.

The [crop-sequence training notebook](../Train%20Duck%20Crop%20Sequence.ipynb) runs a separate candidate model. It checks a bounded sample from that Minnesota feed and evaluates the newest year in counties held out from training. USDA built the field polygons using the full release window, so the test is retrospective. Candidate checkpoints are kept separate from the live assistant. The watcher checks the same feed daily while running. Releases on a new endpoint require a connector update.

To reproduce the original holdout without weight updates, run `python train_rotation.py --device cpu --validate-only .duck-model/rotation/9ff188a769b7c352.pt`. On the A30 use `--device cuda`. Validation checks the dataset fingerprint and reports checkpoint load, retrieval and preparation, and batch inference separately.
